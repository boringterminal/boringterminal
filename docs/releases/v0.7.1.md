## Restore interrupted sessions

- After the background service loses its sessions, **Restore Sessions** brings
  back saved folders, session order, and paired layouts using fresh shells.
- Only visible restored sessions start immediately. Previous commands and
  programs are not resumed or replayed.
- Missing or denied folders offer **Retry** and **Open in Home**, without an
  automatic retry loop. A disconnected viewer offers explicit **Reconnect**.
- Existing sessions survive app upgrades, including sessions owned by the
  v0.6.0 and v0.5.0 background services.

## Fixes and build improvements

- Fixed overlapping title-bar controls on macOS 26 and improved recovery
  banner/button layout in narrow windows.
- Hardened recovery against concurrent viewers, interrupted saves, shutdown
  races, and checkpoint-capacity limits.
- Added certificate-signing options for development and packaging. This
  release remains ad-hoc signed and unnotarized. The reported macOS filesystem
  permission loop remains under investigation; this is not a confirmed TCC fix.

Validation includes native restore/reconnect flows, released-daemon upgrades,
and forced termination during checkpoint writes. Real reboot/power-loss and
certificate-backed consent-retention validation remain pending.
